## How to navigate

Use the sidebar (left) on every wiki page, or follow the links above. Pages are edited continuously — if something is wrong or outdated, click **Edit** at the top of any page. Editing is restricted to collaborators; if you want to contribute and aren't one yet, [open a discussion](https://github.com/hoainho/react-debugger-extension/discussions/new?category=general) introducing yourself.

## Reporting wiki issues

Wiki bugs (typos, broken links, outdated content) → [open an issue](https://github.com/hoainho/react-debugger-extension/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with label `documentation`.

Code/extension bugs → [open an issue](https://github.com/hoainho/react-debugger-extension/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with label `bug`.
